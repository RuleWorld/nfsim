# Acknowledgments

## The original NFsim team

NFsim was created by

- Michael W. Sneddon
- Justin S. Hogg
- Jose-Juan Tapia
- James R. Faeder
- Thierry Emonet

at Yale University and the University of Pittsburgh, with funding from the
National Science Foundation. The method and the original implementation are
described in:

> Sneddon MW, Faeder JR, Emonet T. Efficient modeling, simulation and
> coarse-graining of biological complexity with NFsim. *Nature Methods*
> 8(2):177-183 (2011). https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.1546

Please cite this paper for the network-free simulation method, together with
the version of NFsim you used (see "Citing NFsim" in the README).
